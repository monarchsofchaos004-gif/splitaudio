Getting help is one tap: the "Working on your phone?" card offers both "It works!" and "Report a problem", and "It works!" reports now include the diagnostics, so your device gets a complete row in the compatibility list with you credited. The problem and crash cards take care of themselves (they disappear once the split works again, after 24 hours, or after a week for a crash), and the new-version card stays until you are on the latest version, so no fix is missed; the daily check steps aside when an F-Droid client installed the app.
