Reporting is now one tap and needs no account: "It works!" sends this phone's diagnostics straight to the project's own report endpoint (its code is public in server/), "Report a problem" lets you add a line about what happened first, and the GitHub form stays available (it also opens automatically if sending fails). The two feedback blocks are now a single card: it asks whether the split works once after the first clean 10 minutes, again after each update or after 30 days, and the buttons are always there, so a phone that stops working later can always be reported.
