Stays open on more phones: the background monitor now answers Android right away, which fixes the app closing when you leave the app list (seen on an OPPO with Android 17) and when the system restarts it in the background (seen on an Infinix). Volume keys: on SplitAudio's own screen they adjust the speaker; on the lock screen and when the app in front is silent they adjust your Bluetooth music. Reporting a problem is quicker: tap what went wrong and the details for your phone go with it.
