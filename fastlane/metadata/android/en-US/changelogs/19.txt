Reports now tell the whole story in one tap: "It works!" and "Report a problem" also carry when the split was last active and a short audio-focus snapshot from the router (which apps hold the audio or are playing, the multi-audio-focus setting, the router's last log lines), so "a video pauses my music" or "pause does not stick" can be tracked down without a round of questions. If you tap "It works!" on a phone where the split has not actually run lately, the app tells you how to confirm it (start Shizuku, play music, tap again) and the report is kept as "to be confirmed" until then.
