SplitAudio keeps your music app on the Bluetooth speaker and sends every other app to the phone speaker.

Playing YouTube Music on a Bluetooth speaker, then opening Facebook, Instagram or TikTok, normally sends the video sound to the speaker too. Stock Android has no per-app output setting. SplitAudio adds one: while Bluetooth is connected and the music app is playing, the music app stays on Bluetooth and all other apps use the phone speaker. When the music stops (15 seconds) or Bluetooth disconnects, the rule is removed and audio behaves normally again.

Features
- Music app on Bluetooth, everything else on the phone speaker
- Volume keys: in a speaker-routed app they change the phone speaker volume; in the music app or on the home screen they change Bluetooth as usual
- A video in another app no longer pauses the music (uses Android's built-in "multi audio focus" setting; the app asks you before it switches it on, and you can switch it off again)
- Fail-safe: if the helper process dies, its routing rule dies with it and audio falls back to Bluetooth, never to silence
- Choose your music apps in the app (several are fine); the default is YouTube Music (and the Morphe-patched package)

Status: alpha. Verified on one device only (Xiaomi 15, HyperOS 3, Android 16). The app interface is currently Traditional Chinese.

Requirements
- Shizuku (https://shizuku.rikka.app/), started via wireless debugging. No root needed.
- Android 12 or newer (the volume keys need Android 13).

Permissions
- Shizuku: used to start the small routing helper as the shell user, which is what allows a system-level audio routing rule.
- Accessibility service (optional): only filters the volume keys and reads the foreground app's package name. It does not read screen text.
- Notifications and a foreground service: for the "routing on" status notification and the background monitor.
- Start at boot: to bring the monitor back after a reboot.
- Internet permission: used only for a control port on 127.0.0.1 between the app and its helper on the same phone. Every connection must carry the app's own random token and come from the app itself, so other apps cannot use it. Nothing is ever sent off the phone.

Privacy
Nothing leaves your phone: the Internet permission is only used for 127.0.0.1 (the app talking to its own helper). No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no data collection.
